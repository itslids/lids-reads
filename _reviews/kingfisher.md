Interesting in that it's also an obsession story — but this time the main character is a man who falls for an older woman, which made for a nice juxtaposition with my previous read. That's where the interest ended for me. The main character's emotions felt false, selfish, and lame — emotionally transactional in a way that kept me at a distance the whole way through. The book deals with genuinely heady subjects (fidelity, nontraditional love, death) but at the end I didn't feel fulfilled or enlightened, just wondering what the point was. The prose is genuinely nice — well-written at the sentence level. But nothing stuck. It tries to be grand and impressive in a Donna Tartt kind of way and doesn't get there.
