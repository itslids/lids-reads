The way this author captures the feeling of falling into an obsession with a terrible adult man-baby is wonderful — very immersive and thrilling. The beginning reads like a proper thriller and I loved it for that. But the middle gets slow and repetitive, too much back-and-forth "he's great, he's terrible" when what I really wanted was more exploration of her relationship with her parents — that thread felt rich and underdeveloped. The ending surprised me by going mystical and spiritual, which didn't fit the otherwise concrete tone of the book. I'm not sure if the whole arc was meant to mimic a descent into obsessive madness followed by liberation, but it didn't land for me. I wish it had committed to being a thriller all the way through.
