This was a really entertaining thriller with a great little ending. I thoroughly enjoyed it and thought it was smart, funny, and cringey.

I'm not quite sure why it's getting five-star reviews, though. I wouldn't say it crosses into five-star territory in any way other than entertainment value. It wasn't particularly deep or literary, and it dragged a bit in the middle.
