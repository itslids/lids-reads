This is the third Mona Awad I've read this year and it has a different, sadder tone than *Bunny* or *Rouge* — no spec fic, no genre-bending, just darkly funny and very sharp. The writing and character-building are actually better than *Rouge*, even though I loved that story more. *Bunny* still wins. But what Mona Awad does across all three is write about the feeling of being a smart outsider, having an inner life that isn't what your outer self presents, being a woman set apart from other women. I have never felt more like a main character shares my personality than I do reading her books.
