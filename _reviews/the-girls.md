I did enjoy this somewhat, but I also felt that it was really drawn out and kind of tired. I feel like I've read/seen/consumed a hundred different variations of media about this subject and there was hardly anything original here for me. I'm Manson'd out. I'm cult-girl'd out. I found very little here to be unique, interesting, refreshing, or revelatory, and I don't really understand the hype.

It was quite well written, thus the 3-star review.
