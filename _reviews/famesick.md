Man, I know everyone loves to hate Lena Dunham. I get it. And to be honest, I fell out of love with her somewhere between the last season of *Girls* and like… all the things she said from 2012–2018. But! And I tell you, it is a really surprising "but" for me… I thought this memoir was pretty damn good. I'm shocked that I liked this book.

First of all, no one has millennial alt pixie art girl voice down better than Lena Dunham. She has a shocking amount of self-awareness in some ways, and yet somehow managed to get into the absolute most dysfunctional relationship dynamics at every turn. It was maddening to hear it recounted. And that no one around her said, "Hey! Those people are taking advantage of you, kid." What a screwed up world we live in.

Anyway, the audiobook was fabulously narrated by Lena herself, a voice of a generation of now-forty-somethings.
