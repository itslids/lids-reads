This book absolutely bored me to death. I feel a little guilty about this because the entire thing is an ode to the inner thoughts of driven, accomplished teenage girl athletes. But this was the longest five-hour audiobook of my life.

An irritating side note: The author repeats the boxers' names like a thousand times per paragraph. As if I have reading amnesia.
