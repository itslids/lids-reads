I don't know how to review this book. I found it so clever and laugh-out-loud hilarious in the first third. The middle third was… slow and repetitive. The last third was clever like the beginning but didn't fulfill my expectations.

Still, I found the language in the beginning SO good and SO clever and SO funny that I'm still giving this four stars. Even though I don't really know what it's about, except maybe modern masculinity?
