Beloved is considered literary fiction, but it's also the inspirational seed behind so much excellent modern horror, in my opinion. I could not believe how terrifying some of the scenes in this book were, especially since I didn't come into it thinking it would contain kernels of horror.

I actually read it and also listened to the audiobook read by Toni Morrison, which was a very good combination. It actually took me quite a while to get through it because I kept going back to re-read or re-listen to a particularly well-written or atmospheric piece.

Morrison's turns of phrase and the way she commands language are unmatched: "they were not holding hands, but their shadows were…" Please! An incredible line of prose. Not trite. An originator, Toni Morrison.

I'll carry this dark, frightening, heavy, sickly beautiful book with me for life.
