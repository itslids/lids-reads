Oh boy. I have a lot of thoughts. Like, what did I just read?

And also, some of it was really good. Miranda July describes certain feelings and moments in time exquisitely.

But mostly I just wondered, is this how ridiculous, neurotic, and self-centered all grown-up elder millennial hipsters are? Please no.
