A curator for museums and historical places becomes obsessed with the subject of her latest job.

I loved this book so much but I can hardly explain why. There’s a clever framing device — I don’t want to ruin it — but it worked really well to guide the reader through disjointed stories. No character in this book is really knowable based on what the author gives us, but am I ludicrous to think that lends itself to the entire theme of the book? Curating an environment through which we try to understand, but can never really know, each other and our history?

Wow. I know this is a divisive book and some people will totally hate it, but I found it lovely, refreshing, interesting, and clever as hell.
