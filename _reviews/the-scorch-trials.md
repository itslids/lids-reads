Second in the series, reading it with my kid. We both enjoyed the setting of this book more than the first book, plus new and interesting characters.

Thomas' internal monologues get really repetitive in this book, which made it a little bit of a slog.

Overall, still fun and twisty YA. The girl characters are kinda flat.
