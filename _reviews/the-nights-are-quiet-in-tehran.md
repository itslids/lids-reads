This was a beautiful book spanning several decades from the perspectives of five family members: two parents who were revolutionaries in Iran in 1979, then fled to Germany when the Mullahs took over; and their three children who all see the world from different perspectives based in part on when they departed Iran and arrived in Germany. Compelling stream-of-consciousness narration, first person for each individual character, ending on a hopeful note. This is another short-list contender for the International Booker Prize -- I bet it will win.
