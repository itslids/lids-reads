It's wild to imagine becoming an internationally recognized open-source researcher and weapons expert from your kitchen, with a young family, barely making ends meet. This was a straightforward recounting of events, but I kept getting lost in the non-linear timeline. I listened to the audiobook which might have made this worse. It was still a great origin story.
