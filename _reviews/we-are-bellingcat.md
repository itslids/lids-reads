It's wild to imagine becoming an internationally recognized open-source researcher and weapons expert from your kitchen, with a young family, barely making ends meet.

This was a straightforward recounting of events, but I got a little confused by the timeline, because each chapter is its own separate story and they sometimes overlap. I listened to the audiobook, which might have made this worse. The confusion made me bored, which made it hard to finish.

It was still a great origin story. Anyone who's ever done any kind of investigative work will appreciate it.
