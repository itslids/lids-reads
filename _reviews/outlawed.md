The concept for this was so fun: dystopian western where the badass outlaws are shes and theys! There were really good moments, but the exposition was clunky and the tone was too glossy and shallow.

"Cinematic" is a good description, though. I want to watch this movie starring E.R. Fightmaster and Merritt Wever.
