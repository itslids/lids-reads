# About Lids Reads

I'm Lindsey — Lids to most people. This is where I write about what I'm reading: the classics I keep coming back to (Dickens, Austen, the Brontës, Ford Madox Ford), the new books people press into my hands, and whatever ends up on the nightstand in between.

## How I rate

| Stars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | Changed how I see something. I'll reread it. |
| ★★★★ | Excellent. I'm recommending it to people. |
| ★★★ | Good, with reservations. |
| ★★ | Didn't work for me, but I can see who it's for. |
| ★ | Not for me. |

Half stars happen.
